Empty tools teach nothing. That's why first boot offers the expedition:
Mars or Bust, a complete worked desk — two survivors, a dog, two hundred
files of surviving and shipbuilding, and the fifty cards it takes to fly.

## Read it like a case study

Every pattern this blog preaches is in there working: cards referencing
field notes by line, agent comments signed in threads, honest done
statuses (the freezer repair was `abandoned`; the radio vigil, `not
relevant`), boards per workstream.

## Ride it before you trust it

Open MARS-3's case file. Search "water" and watch exact and meaning hits
land. Press `w` and see the expedition's web — the flight plan is a hub;
the almanac feeds the seasonal cards.

![The expedition's web in the Overview](/said-and-done/shots/overview.png)

_Fifty cards, two hundred files, one visible structure — a working desk to dissect._

## Then delete it, or don't

It never counts against the free plan, and it deletes cleanly. But most
people keep it around — half tutorial, half proof that a desk can hold a
whole hard project without lying about any of it.
