Lists hide structure. The Overview draws it: every card, every file it
touches, one living web that answers questions lists can't.

## Hubs tell you what matters

The note with six cards hanging off it is load-bearing — treat it with
respect before editing. The tooltip lists exactly which cards lean on it.

## Orphans tell you what's drifting

Files with no cards: thinking without commitments. Cards with no files:
commitments without thinking. Both are fine — until they aren't. The web
shows the ratio at a glance.

![The Overview with a tooltip listing a card's files](/said-and-done/shots/overview-light.png)

_Hover any node: the other side of every link, named._

## Use it before big moves

Reorganizing a project? Open the web first. The clusters you see are the
real subprojects — often not the ones your folder names claim. Drag nodes
to play; the web settles into what your work actually looks like.
