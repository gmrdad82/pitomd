Every abandoned Trello board died the same death: it described work that
lived somewhere else, and the description drifted until updating it felt
like a second job.

## The fix is gravity, not discipline

Said and Done. cards reference the files the work lives in — path and line
range. When the board points at the work, keeping it honest stops being a
chore; the card is just the file's public face.

## Migrate by intent, not by import

Don't port the graveyard. Open your old board once, and re-say the ten
cards that still matter — `n`, your own words, a reference each. The other
ninety were already dead; give them the funeral they earned.

![The board with cards anchored into markdown](/said-and-done/shots/board.png)

_Cards with gravity: each one points at the lines it answers to._

## What keeps it alive

The folder IS the project — new files appear in search automatically, the
Overview shows which notes feed which cards, and the desk runs where your
editor runs. The board stops being a mirror you polish and becomes a lens
you look through.
