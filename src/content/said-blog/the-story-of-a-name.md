---
title: "Said and Done: the story of a name"
description: "Why the app is called Said — and why the tagline was there before the name was."
pubDate: 2026-08-24
---

Products usually get named after what they hold: Notes, Tasks, Boards. We
named ours after what happens at a desk that works: things get said, and
then they get done.

A card in Said is a spoken thing. You say it in your own words, it takes a
number, and from that moment the desk keeps your word for you — who said
what, when it moved, how it ended. The history isn't a log; it's a record of
speech. Comments are called what they are: things people said.

That's why the tagline came first. "Said and Done" is the whole product in
three words — the distance between a thought in a note and a card marked
done, made as short as we could make it.

The mark tells the same story: three spoken lines, and the last one answered
with a check. The freshest word, lit.

Said is made by Pito, the same small house behind Pito Studio — one person's
tools, built for people who write things down and mean them.
