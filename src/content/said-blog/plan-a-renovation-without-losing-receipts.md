A renovation is three months of decisions made in hardware store aisles.
The receipts, quotes, and measurements scatter faster than the dust.

## Cards that hold evidence

Each workstream is a card: the bathroom, the wiring, the floor. Attach
photos of quotes and befores straight onto cards — files are copied in
content-addressed, so two `IMG_0001.jpg` never collide, and the originals
stay wherever your phone put them.

## Measurements live in notes, cards point at them

One `measurements.md`, updated as you go. Cards reference the lines they
need: the vanity card points at the alcove width, the flooring card at the
square meters. In the store, the remote client shows the same cards from
your phone, through your own tunnel.

![The remote client on a phone — the same desk from anywhere](/said-and-done/shots/remote/r-card.png)

_The card in the aisle: measurements, attachments, and the thread, on your phone._

## When the contractor says "you approved this"

Case file. Born date, the quote attached, the thread where scope changed.
Renovations are disputes waiting for records — keep the records.
