Journaling apps ask you to narrate your day after living it. The dossier
writes the first draft while you work.

## Work normally; the record accrues

Cards born, moved, finished; comments dropped; references added. The
dossier orders it all, freshest first, dated. At day's end you're reading
your day, not reconstructing it.

## Add the human margin

One comment on the day's main card — how it actually went. Thirty
seconds. The machine record plus one honest margin note beats an empty
journal template every evening.

## Fridays, read the week

Scroll the week in the dossier before the review. Patterns show up that
memory smooths over: the Tuesday everything stalled, the card that moved
four times. The diary you didn't write, ready to be read.
