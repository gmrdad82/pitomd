Launches fail in the gaps between checklists — the step everyone thought
someone else owned.

## The runbook is a chapter, not a doc

Every launch step is a page in order: freeze, tag, build, publish,
announce. Each references the runbook note lines with the exact commands.
The record IS the state of the launch — no separate status thread to
maintain.

## Agents work the mechanical middle

Over MCP, your release agent can move its own pages and comment results —
signed. You watch statuses, not logs. Anything red is a page that stopped
moving.

![The record as the single source of launch truth](/said-and-done/shots/board-general.png)

_Backlog to done is the launch — anyone can read where it stands._

## The retro writes itself

Next morning, the record holds the whole night in order. Rate the chapter,
file the two pages that were missing, and next launch inherits a runbook
that actually happened.
