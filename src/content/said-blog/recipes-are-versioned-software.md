Anyone who cooks seriously runs an R&D lab with terrible record-keeping.
"What did I change last time?" is the kitchen's oldest unsolved bug.

## The recipe is a note; attempts are cards

`sourdough.md` holds the living recipe. Each bake is a card: "Bake #9 —
75% hydration," referencing the recipe lines being tested. The result goes
in the thread, with a photo attached.

## Honest endings, delicious data

`done` for keepers — merge the tweak into the note. `abandoned` for the
rye experiment nobody ate. The record of failures is half the value: bake
#4's thread saves you from repeating it in November.

because meaning search doesn't need you to remember what you named it.
