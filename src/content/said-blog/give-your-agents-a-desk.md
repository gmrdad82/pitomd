The difference between an AI tool and an AI teammate is a surface where
its work can be seen, questioned, and traced. A chat log isn't that. A
board is.

## One line, and the agent sits down

While the desk is open, an MCP door answers on localhost. `claude mcp add`
one line and your agent can list boards, search notes, file cards, move
them, and speak in threads — with the same tools you use, no more.

## Every action signed

Agents act under their own names — `agent:quartermaster`, in the history,
in the threads, next to yours. When a card moved and you didn't move it,
the case file says exactly who did.

![A case file where an agent reports under its own name](/said-and-done/shots/casefile.png)

_The report is in the thread, signed. Accountability is what makes delegation real._

## What to actually delegate

Sweeps: "file a card per TODO in this folder." Triage: "move everything
mentioning the old API to the migration board." Reporting: "comment your
findings on MARS-7." The desk keeps the record; you keep the judgment.
