Trip plans are perfect until the first delayed train. Then the plan needs
to bend without losing its receipts.

## Before: the chapter fills up

Cards per booking, per leg, per must-see. Confirmation PDFs attach
straight to pages; notes hold the research ("the museum's closed Tuesdays"
— referenced by the museum page).

## During: the phone carries the desk

The remote client rides your own tunnel — the same pages on the platform
when the train dies. Move "Day 3: Alhambra" back to todo, file "rebook
leg" with the confirmation number in the title.

![The remote client's record on a phone](/said-and-done/shots/remote/r-board-light.png)

_Replanning at the platform — same desk, smaller glass._

## After: the trip becomes a note

The record is already the trip report: what happened, in order, with the
receipts attached. Next trip's research starts by searching this one.
