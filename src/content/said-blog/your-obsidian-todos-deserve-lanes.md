If your vault has `TODO` scattered through forty files, you don't have a
task system — you have a diary with regrets.

## Keep the habit, add a desk

Nothing about your notes changes: same folder, same files, same TODO
lines. Point Said and Done. at the folder. Now write a card for the ones
you mean — title in your words, reference on the TODO's line.

## One search over both worlds

`/` searches cards and notes in one stroke: exact words first, meaning
underneath. The TODO you wrote in March about "the migration mess"
surfaces even when you search "database cleanup."

![The finder over cards and notes at once](/said-and-done/shots/finder-light.png)

_Cards and raw notes answer together — the desk reads along with you._

## Lanes give the part notes can't

A note can hold an intention; it can't hold _state_. Backlog, todo, in
progress, done — with honest endings for the rest. Your vault keeps the
thinking; the board finally shows the doing.
