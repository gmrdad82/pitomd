Launches fail in the gaps between checklists — the step everyone thought
someone else owned.

## The runbook is a board, not a doc

Every launch step is a card in order: freeze, tag, build, publish,
announce. Each references the runbook note lines with the exact commands.
The board IS the state of the launch — no separate status thread to
maintain.

## Agents work the mechanical middle

Over MCP, your release agent can move its own cards and comment results —
signed. You watch lanes, not logs. Anything red is a card that stopped
moving.

![The board as the single source of launch truth](/said-and-done/shots/board-general.png)

_Left to right is the launch — anyone can read where it stands._

## The retro writes itself

Next morning, the dossier holds the whole night in order. Rate the board,
file the two cards that were missing, and next launch inherits a runbook
that actually happened.
