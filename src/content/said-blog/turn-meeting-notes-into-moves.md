Every meeting produces two artifacts: a warm feeling of alignment, and a
markdown file nobody reads again. The second one is fixable.

## Write the notes as you always do

No format, no discipline. One file per meeting in the notebook's folder.
Said and Done. indexes it the moment it lands — exact words and meaning both.

## File the moves before you stand up

While the meeting is closing, `n` a page per decision — title in your own
words. Reference the lines of the meeting note that decided it: the page
carries `meeting-2026-08-21.md#L14-19`, readable in place with `r`.

![A reference read in place — the exact markdown lines rendered inside the page](/said-and-done/shots/reference.png)

_The decision and its context, one keypress apart — no tab switching, no hunting._

## When someone asks "why are we doing this?"

The page answers with its reference. The record answers with the whole
week — every page as one running line, freshest on top. Meetings stop
being a memory sport.
