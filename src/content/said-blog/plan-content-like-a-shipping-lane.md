A content calendar in a spreadsheet knows dates. It doesn't know drafts,
references, or the difference between "idea" and "committed."

## Ideas are cheap cards

Every idea gets a backlog card the moment it occurs — one line, `n`, done.
Once a week, promote the few worth writing into todo. The backlog being
long is a feature: it's a compost heap, not a promise.

## Drafts live as files, cards track their state

Write in markdown in the project folder. The card references the draft;
the lanes carry it from outline to published. When a piece cites an older
note, the reference records the lineage.

![The dossier reading the pipeline as a record](/said-and-done/shots/dossier.png)

_What shipped, what stalled, what died — the pipeline's honest history._

## Publishing cadence without pressure

The dossier shows your real cadence, not your aspirational one. Rate the
board monthly; when the stars stall, the record shows exactly where —
usually between draft and edit, where content goes to die.
