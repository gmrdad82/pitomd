Retrospectives fail by being meetings. A board rating is a retrospective
compressed to one gesture — and only you can move it.

## Rate on rhythm, not on mood

End of each week: `shift+u` or `shift+d`, half a star at a time. The
stars aren't a grade of you — they're the board's health: is this surface
still telling the truth about the work?

## Low stars diagnose structure

A board stuck at two stars has a shape problem: lanes too coarse, cards
too big, or a project that's actually three. Split it — cards keep their
ids and histories when they move.

![The board with its rating row](/said-and-done/shots/board.png)

_Five stars is not the goal; honesty is. The stars just make drift visible._

## The record of the ratings

Ratings live with the board, and your dossier remembers the weeks. Watching
a board climb from two to four stars is watching a system get honest — the
cheapest retrospective program ever run.
