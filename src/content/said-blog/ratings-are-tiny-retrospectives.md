Retrospectives fail by being meetings. A chapter rating is a retrospective
compressed to one gesture — and only you can move it.

## Rate on rhythm, not on mood

End of each week: `shift+u` or `shift+d`, half a star at a time. The
stars aren't a grade of you — they're the chapter's health: is this record
still telling the truth about the work?

## Low stars diagnose structure

A chapter stuck at two stars has a shape problem: pages too big, or a
notebook that's actually three. Split it — pages keep their ids and
histories when they move.

![The record with its rating row](/said-and-done/shots/board.png)

_Five stars is not the goal; honesty is. The stars just make drift visible._

## The record of the ratings

Ratings live with the chapter, and the record remembers the weeks.
Watching a chapter climb from two to four stars is watching a system get
honest — the cheapest retrospective program ever run.
