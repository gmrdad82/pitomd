Course prep has a dirty secret: you rebuild the same semester every year
because last year's decisions are buried in last year's folders.

## The semester is a chapter

One notebook per course. A chapter per term. Pages per lecture, lab, and
assignment — each referencing the source materials: slides outline lines,
reading list sections, the assignment spec. `w` opens the Overview and
shows which materials feed which lectures — one living web.

![The Overview — pages and files as one web](/said-and-done/shots/overview.png)

_Which lecture leans on which notes — visible at a glance before the term starts._

## Teach from the record

During the term, the record holds the running account: what was covered,
what slipped, which lab needs a second session next year. Notes filed in
page threads, dated automatically.

## Next year is a chapter away

New term, new chapter, same notebook. The pages that worked move over with
their references intact. The ones that flopped sit in done with an honest
`not relevant`. Your course compounds instead of resetting.
