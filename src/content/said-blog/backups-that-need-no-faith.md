Backup features usually mean an export button and hope. This desk backs up
like infrastructure: automatically, verifiably, restorably.

## What happens without you

Once a day while open, the database snapshots via a proper online backup —
no torn writes — gzipped beside a tar of every attachment. Fourteen days
kept. Jobs shows the last run; a Back up now button exists for the nervous.

## Restore is file copying

New machine: install, copy the backup in, point at your notes folder.
Notes were never the app's hostage — they're your markdown, in your
folder, under your own git if you like.

![Jobs and the desk's own semaphore](/said-and-done/shots/board-general.png)

_The desk runs its own maintenance and shows its work — no faith required._

## Test it once

The only backup that counts is one you've restored. Twenty minutes, one
laptop, one coffee — then never wonder again.
