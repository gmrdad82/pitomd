Language apps optimize for the streak. Actual acquisition is messier:
sources, drills, and a thousand tiny confusions that deserve filing.

## Notes are your corpus

Vocabulary lists, grammar confusions, transcripts of things you actually
wanted to say — markdown, one folder, indexed for exact and meaning search
the moment you save.

## Cards are commitments, not flashcards

"Shadow episode 12," "write 200 words about the trip," "clear the
subjunctive confusion note." Real work, walked left to right weekly. The
board doesn't gamify; it just refuses to let intentions blur.

![The board holding the week's language work](/said-and-done/shots/board-light.png)

_A week of real practice — visible, movable, finishable._

## Meaning search is a tutor's memory

"How did I explain that verb aspect thing to myself in March?" — meaning
search finds your own explanation, which beats anyone else's, because it
was built from your confusion.
