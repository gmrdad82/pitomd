Read-later apps are where articles go to feel saved. The failure isn't
storage — it's that saving and _intending_ look identical.

## Split the two on sight

Notes hold what you read: one markdown file per book or long piece,
highlights and thoughts inline. Cards hold what you intend: "apply the
spaced-repetition chapter to the course deck," referencing the exact
highlight lines.

## The backlog is allowed to be honest

Most reading intentions die. Good. Move them to done as `not relevant`
without shame — the pipeline stays believable, and the ten that survive
get real attention.

![The finder mixing exact and meaning hits](/said-and-done/shots/finder.png)

_"That argument about compounding" — findable even when you forgot the author's words._

## Meaning search is the payoff

Months later, the idea resurfaces without its label. Meaning search over
your own highlights finds it — computed on your GPU, sent nowhere. Your
reading becomes a private search engine of things you actually thought.
