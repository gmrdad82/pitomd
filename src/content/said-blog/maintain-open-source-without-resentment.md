Public issue trackers are inboxes other people fill. Maintainers burn out
managing _their_ queue instead of _the project's_ direction.

## The private chapter is the strategy

Upstream issues stay where they are. Your desk holds the pages YOU choose:
the refactor that unlocks three fixes, the doc rewrite that kills a
recurring question. Each references your design notes, not the noise.

## Honest endings protect the hours

`not relevant` is the maintainer's shield: filed, considered, declined,
recorded. The chapter's done pages become the changelog of your actual
attention.

![The record as the project's private account](/said-and-done/shots/dossier-light.png)

_What you actually decided, in order — the story the public tracker never tells._

## Agents on triage duty

An MCP agent can pre-sort: file pages for issues matching your interest
areas, referencing the relevant design notes. It signs its suggestions;
you keep the veto. The queue serves you, finally.
