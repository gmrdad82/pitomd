A novel is a project with ten thousand moving pieces and exactly one
person on the hook. The trick is making the pieces visible without making
the writing feel like project management.

## Scenes are pages, the manuscript stays yours

The manuscript lives as markdown in the notebook folder — one file per
book chapter, or per scene, however you write. Pages carry the _work_:
"rewrite the ferry scene," referencing `chapter-07.md#L120-186`. The words
stay in your files; the desk only points at them.

## A chapter per draft pass

"Draft two," "Continuity," "Kill your darlings" — each pass its own
chapter. Pages move between them without losing their number — MARS-12
stays MARS-12 through every reorganization, and its history remembers each
move.

![The record — every page as one running line](/said-and-done/shots/dossier-light.png)

_The record reads like the book's black box: what was decided, when, and why._

## Meaning search earns its keep

"Where did I mention the lighthouse keeper's daughter?" Exact search finds
the phrase; meaning search finds the scene where she's only _the girl on
the rocks_. Both run at home, on your GPU, over your own files.
