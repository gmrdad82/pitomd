---
title: "The dossier: reading a card like a case file"
description: "Boards are for deciding. When you need to understand, the same cards read better as one record."
pubDate: 2026-08-27
---

A board answers one question well: what should move next? But some mornings
the question is different. What happened to this? Why did it come back? How
long has this really been open?

Columns are the wrong shape for those questions. So Said has a second face:
the dossier. Same cards, same data, no sync — one running record with the
freshest word on top, like a ledger of the desk.

Click any card in the dossier and it opens as a case file. The vitals: what
state it's in, how many times it came back from done, how long it lived, who
spoke in it. The spec trail: every edit to what the card asks. The activity:
the whole journey between lanes. The thread: everything said, by everyone,
named. And the reopens — called out in their own section, because a card
that keeps coming back is trying to tell you something.

No one owns a card in Said, so the case file is also how a shared card stays
honest. When you and an agent both worked it, the file shows exactly who did
what, in order, without anyone's memory in the loop.

Deciding and understanding are different jobs. Now they have different
faces — and a switch in Settings, plus one question at first boot: how
should your work face you?
