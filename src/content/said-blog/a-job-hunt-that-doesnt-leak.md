A job search is a pipeline with feelings. Spreadsheets track it coldly and
lose the story; your memory keeps the story and loses the dates.

## One notebook, four statuses

Backlog: roles worth a look. Todo: applying this week. In progress: anything
with a human on the other end. Done: offers, rejections, and the ones you
walked away from — with the honest status each deserves: done, abandoned, or
not relevant.

## Notes per company, references per page

Keep a markdown file per company — role links, salary notes, interview
prep. Every application page references the lines that matter. Before a
call, open the page, hit `r`, and the prep is in front of you.

![The finder searching across every page and note](/said-and-done/shots/finder-light.png)

_"Who mentioned equity refreshers?" — one search across pages and notes both._

## The part nobody else should host

Compensation notes and interview impressions are not SaaS material. This
desk keeps them in a local database and a folder of markdown you can read
with `cat`. Nothing leaves the machine.
