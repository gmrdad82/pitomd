Scope creep is rarely malice. It's amnesia: nobody remembers what was
agreed, so the loudest memory wins.

## A project per client, a board per engagement

Client folder in, project out. The engagement board's backlog IS the
scope — cards born from the proposal, referencing its lines. Change
requests are new cards referencing the email note that asked.

## Decisions get threads

"Client approved the extra revision round" is a comment on the card, dated.
Six weeks later that thread is the difference between an awkward call and
a calm one.

![A card wearing its references](/said-and-done/shots/card-light.png)

_The scope line it came from, one keypress away — receipts without ceremony._

## Invoicing reads the done lane

At month's end, the done lane with `done_status` filters is your invoice
draft: what shipped, what was dropped by agreement, what was out of scope.
The case files back every line item.
