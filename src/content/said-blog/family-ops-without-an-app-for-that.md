Family logistics is a real operations job running on sticky notes and one
partner's memory. It deserves a desk, not another subscription.

## One notebook called Home

Folders: school, house, health, trips. Every form deadline, boiler
service, and passport renewal is a page. The statuses tell the truth at a
glance: what's waiting, what's this week, what's actually in motion.

## Notes for the stuff you re-learn yearly

The boiler's service quirks, the school's term calendar, the sizes
everyone wears — markdown notes, referenced by the pages that need them.
Next year's page points at the same note; you never re-research it.

![A page carrying its references and attachments](/said-and-done/shots/card.png)

_The form itself, attached; the deadline, in the title; the context, referenced._

## The remote is the errand mode

At the store or the school office, the phone shows the same record through
your own tunnel. Check the page, tick it done, and the desk at home
already knows.
